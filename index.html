The Concept

More Than Just Words

Understanding language through expression and creativity

An idiom is a phrase whose meaning is different from the literal meaning of its individual words. Idioms make language more expressive, engaging and meaningful. This exhibition presents twenty English idioms through visual posters and learning activities, helping visitors understand how these expressions are used in everyday communication.
