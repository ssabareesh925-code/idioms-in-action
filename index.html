ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

Language is more than words.

Idioms are expressions whose meanings are different from the literal meanings of the individual words. They add colour, personality and depth to everyday communication.

IDIOMS IN ACTION is an English exhibition project created by Team 07. It presents 20 selected idioms through visual posters, a written report, presentations and learning activities.

Our aim is to encourage vocabulary development, creative thinking and confident use of English in everyday situations.

Understand the expression. Discover the meaning.

Every idiom tells a story. By connecting expressions with situations and examples, we make learning English more engaging, memorable and meaningful.

IDIOMS IN ACTION · TEAM 07

The Final Deadline

A dramatic presentation demonstrating the 20 selected English idioms through everyday college-life situations.

PRESENTED BY

Sakthivel S · Register No. 721026205033

Characters

1. Sakthivel – Team Leader

2. Arun – The Procrastinator

3. Kavin – The Hard Worker

4. Rahul – The Creative Thinker

The other performers' names can be changed according to the participating team members.

Introduction

Good morning everyone! I am Sakthivel S, and today we are presenting a mini-skit titled “The Final Deadline – A Journey Through 20 Idioms.” An idiom is an expression whose meaning is different from the literal meanings of its individual words. Idioms make communication more interesting and expressive. Through this drama, we will demonstrate 20 selected English idioms in a college-life situation. Let's begin!

Scene 1 – The Project Announcement

The students are sitting in a classroom discussing their project submission.

Sakthivel: Guys, our project submission is approaching. We need to get the ball rolling immediately!

Arun: I know, but I haven't even started studying the topic!

Kavin: Then you better hit the books today. We don't have much time.

Rahul: Don't worry. Learning something new always has a learning curve. We can learn step by step.

Sakthivel: First, let's divide the work. Everyone should be on the same page.

Arun: Okay! I'll try to understand everything. I'm still learning the ropes of project work.

Scene 2 – The Creative Plan

The team discusses how to make its presentation special.

Rahul: Instead of preparing an ordinary presentation, let's think outside the box and create something different.

Kavin: That's a good idea. We should raise the bar and make our presentation better.

Sakthivel: Exactly! Everyone should go the extra mile to make this successful.

Arun: But I think getting the first prize is a long shot!

Rahul: Don't decide the result before trying. We need to think on our feet when unexpected problems happen.

Sakthivel: Correct. Let's work together. We are all in the same boat.

Scene 3 – The Unexpected Problem

The team discovers that its presentation file is not working.

Arun: Oh no! The presentation file is not opening!

Kavin: What happened? Did you save the correct file?

Arun: I thought I did!

Rahul: Wait. The file name is different, and the last modified time is yesterday. Let's put two and two together. Maybe we're opening the old version.

Sakthivel: Good observation. We should keep our eyes peeled for mistakes like this.

Kavin: Unfortunately, the current version is corrupted. Looks like we're going back to the drawing board.

Rahul: Don't panic. Let's try another format. We can use trial and error to find what works.

Scene 4 – The Night Before Submission

The students continue preparing their presentation late at night.

Kavin: It's already midnight! We are going to burn the midnight oil tonight.

Arun: I am tired, but I don't want to give up now.

Sakthivel: We have to get your act together and finish the remaining work.

Rahul: Remember, we should never cut corners just to finish quickly. Quality matters.

Arun: I understand. I'll correct the mistakes and check everything carefully.

Scene 5 – The Final Success

The next morning, the team is ready to submit its project.

Sakthivel: Is everything ready?

Rahul: Presentation completed!

Kavin: Documentation completed!

Arun: I have finished the final corrections!

Sakthivel: Excellent! We completed everything at the eleventh hour.

Arun: This project taught me that preparation and teamwork are more important than last-minute panic.

Rahul: Exactly. We faced problems, learned new things and found solutions together.

Kavin: We should always remember that success comes from effort, creativity and determination.

Sakthivel: And with the right attitude, the sky's the limit!

ALL TOGETHER: THANK YOU!

All 20 Idioms Demonstrated

  1. Think Outside the Box – Think creatively.
  2. Hit the Books – Start studying seriously.
  3. Learn the Ropes – Learn how something works.
  4. Back to the Drawing Board – Start again after an unsuccessful attempt.
  5. On the Same Page – Have the same understanding.
  6. Go the Extra Mile – Make more effort than expected.
  7. Get the Ball Rolling – Start an activity.
  8. In the Same Boat – Be in the same situation.
  9. Burn the Midnight Oil – Work late into the night.
  10. A Learning Curve – The process of learning something new.
  11. Think on Your Feet – React quickly to a situation.
  12. Trial and Error – Find a solution through repeated attempts.
  13. Cut Corners – Do something carelessly to save time or effort.
  14. Raise the Bar – Set a higher standard.
  15. Keep Your Eyes Peeled – Watch carefully and stay alert.
  16. A Long Shot – Something with a small chance of success.
  17. Get Your Act Together – Become organized and responsible.
  18. Put Two and Two Together – Understand by connecting information.
  19. At the Eleventh Hour – At the last possible moment.
  20. The Sky's the Limit – There is no limit to what can be achieved.

Conclusion

Through this mini-skit, we demonstrated all 20 idioms selected by Team 07 in a practical and entertaining way. Instead of simply memorizing meanings, we showed how idioms can be used naturally in everyday conversations. Learning becomes more meaningful when we use language in real-life situations.

Thank you!
